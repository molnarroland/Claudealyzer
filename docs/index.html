Features

From the whole month down to a single tool callnpm test

Each view answers one question. You can move between them in one click or with a keyboard shortcut.

History

Browse every session like a chat

Projects → sessions → conversation. Each turn shows your prompt, the answer, and the work in between, collapsed into one readable list.

  • Thinking, shell commands, file reads and edits, searches, web and MCP calls
  • Subagents open inline with their own steps, usage and cost
  • Peak context and cost on every turn, plus a red dot on sessions with high findings
  • Sort sessions by recency or by cost, and filter by agent
Step detail

What one tool call really did

Click any step to see the full command or input and the full output, along with what the step cost you.

  • Wall time and its share of the turn
  • Tokens it added to the context, and how many later calls re-sent them
  • How often the same step was repeated, in the turn and in the session
  • The findings that point at this step
Session analysis

Why this session was expensive

One page that explains a session: where the money went, which turn blew up the context, and what took the time.

  • Cost per turn, stacked by input, cache write, cache read and output
  • Peak context per turn against your threshold
  • Time split between model, tests, tools and subagents
  • Ranked cost drivers with an estimated impact and a link to the evidence
Usage & insights panel

Findings next to the conversation

The right-hand panel follows your selection: the whole session, one turn, or one subagent. You get its tokens, list price and share of your plan, plus the findings that apply.

  • Every finding comes with a concrete suggestion
  • "Go to turn" jumps straight to the evidence
  • Findings that repeat are grouped, and the worst one is shown first
Cost explained

List price vs. what you actually pay

On a subscription, "$2.33" means something different than on an API key. This view shows both numbers and explains the math.

  • List price, what it cost you, and a value multiplier for your plan
  • The full calculation for each token type: tokens × rate
  • Scenarios: no caching, a cheaper model, the Batch API, or a custom price factor
  • Billing period so far vs. your plan fee, projected to the renewal date

A daily cost chart for each agent and a sortable table of every session, for one project or a whole source.

  • 7 days, 30 days or all time
  • Finding badges on every row
  • Average and median cost per session
Calendar

A month view coloured by cost, prompts or tokens. Click a day to open it.

Sources & settings

Your folders, your billing, your prices

Agent folders are found automatically on first start. You can add more at any time, such as a second profile or a backup.

  • Billing per source: API key with discount, subscription, team seat or free tier
  • Prices are stored by date, so a price change never rewrites old sessions
  • Every insight threshold can be edited
  • Pick the look and the color scheme, or follow the system

Insights

Concrete suggestions, ranked by dollars

Every session, turn and subagent is checked against these rules. Each finding has a severity, an estimated impact and a fix. All thresholds can be changed.

High

Context too large

Turns that re-send a huge cached context on every call.

High

Slow test & build runs

Commands that take up most of a turn's wall time.

Medium

Long session, no compaction

Context kept growing instead of being compacted.

Medium

Large tool output

Single tool results that bloat every later call.

Medium

Many files / files read again

Broad or repeated file reading.

Medium

Errors and retries

Failing commands in a row.

Medium

Repeated command

The same command, run again and again.

Medium

CPU-heavy commands

Builds, bundlers and containers that keep the machine busy.

Medium

Subagent fan-out

Subagents that cost more than they return.

Medium

An expensive model doing simple reads and edits.

Medium

How it counts

Numbers you can check

No magic multipliers. Every figure comes from the tokens in your transcripts and a pricing table you can see and edit.

  1. Each API response is counted once

    Claude by message.id, Codex by response_id, Gemini by message id, even when a response shows up several times in a transcript.

  2. Usage is normalised

    Uncached input, cache write (5 min / 1 h), cache read and output, including thinking. For Codex and Gemini, uncached = input − cached.

  3. List price uses the rate on that day

    Σ tokens × rate per model, using the rate that applied on the day of each request. Unknown models are shown as price unknown, never as $0.

  4. Your cost follows your billing

    API key: list price minus your discount. Subscription or seat: plan fee × this usage's share of the billing period. Free tier: $0.

Costs are estimates based on public list prices. Use them to compare sessions and spot waste, not as an invoice.

Native everywhere

Looks at home on every OS

On a Mac you get a Mac app, on Windows a Windows app and on Linux a GNOME/GTK app, in light or dark mode, in your system's accent color.

Go

Go + Wails v3

The parser, index, pricing and insight engine are written in Go. Wails ships them as one small native binary.

⌘

The OS's own web view

No bundled Chromium. The UI runs in the renderer the OS already has: WebKit on macOS, WebKitGTK on Linux and WebView2 on Windows. That makes it small and quick to start, with a light memory footprint.

S

Spartan Native

Spartan Native is my native-look theme for spartan/ui. One Angular codebase gets the geometry, materials and behaviour of each OS, and it reads your real accent color, color scheme, contrast and text size.

Looks: Windows 11 · Windows 10 · macOS 26 · macOS 13–15 · GNOME · KDE Plasma · ChromeOS

Privacy

Your transcripts never leave your machine

Claudealyzer has no server, no account and no telemetry. It only reads the agents' folders and keeps its own small config and cache next to your other app data. Delete those folders and it's gone without a trace.

Config & billing<config dir>/claudealyzer/config.json
Edited prices<config dir>/claudealyzer/pricing.json
Summary cache<cache dir>/claudealyzer/index.json
